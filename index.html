Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a bike in Pula?

A regular bike costs 15 € for 3 hours or 22 € for one day. Multi-day rentals are cheaper per day: 17 € per day for 2 days, 15 € per day for 3 days and 13 € per day for 4 or more days. E-bikes start at 33 € for 3 hours or 39 € per day, e-scooters at 11 € for 30 minutes or 34 € per day. Children's bikes are 50% of the regular price.

How do I book?

Choose the dates and what you want to rent on the Book Now page. You will see the price straight away. Send us the request by WhatsApp or e-mail and we confirm availability. You pay when you pick up the equipment.

Do I pay online?

No. There is no online payment. You pay on arrival, by card (Mastercard, Visa) or in cash.

Where can I rent a bike in Pula and Medulin?

We have three locations: Verudela (near the Aquarium) and Camp Arena Stoja in Pula, and Camp Arena Medulin in Medulin.

Is a helmet and lock included?

Yes. Every rental includes a helmet and a lock free of charge.

Do I need to leave a deposit?

No deposit is required. You only need to show a valid photo ID (identity card, passport or driver's licence) when you pick up your rental.

Can I rent an e-bike or e-scooter?

Yes, e-bikes and e-scooters are available at all three locations. E-bikes are perfect for longer rides to Cape Kamenjak or along the Medulin coast.

Do you rent SUP boards and kayaks?

Yes, SUP boards and kayaks are available at Camp Arena Stoja in Pula.

Can children rent bikes?

Yes. Children's bikes are 50% of the regular price. Child seats cost 4 € per day.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is free for bike and e-scooter rentals of 2 or more days, or for bookings of 4 or more vehicles. Mention your accommodation in the request.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, but in July and August we recommend sending a request in advance so we can keep the equipment for you.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 9 AM to 9 PM at all locations.

What if the bike breaks down?

We provide free support on the road. Call us and we will repair or replace your bike as quickly as possible anywhere in the Pula and Medulin area.

What if it rains?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge if the weather makes riding unsafe.

Can I return the bike to a different location?

Yes. You can pick up at Verudela and return at Camp Stoja or Camp Arena Medulin – just let us know in advance.

Do you offer guided tours?

Yes. We run guided tours from Verudela to Cape Kamenjak and to the Šijana forest, plus sunrise and sunset rides. Private tours for families and groups are available on request.
